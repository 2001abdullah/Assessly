# Assessly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

Assessly processes account details, exam definitions, answer keys, scanned OMR
answers, student identifiers entered on answer sheets, and calculated results
to provide assessment and grading features.

Uploaded answer-sheet images are used only for OMR processing and are deleted
from temporary server storage after processing. Structured scan data and exam
results are stored in the configured PostgreSQL database until the account
owner deletes the relevant exam or account.

Assessly does not sell personal data. Data is shared only with infrastructure
providers needed to operate the service, such as hosting, database, monitoring,
and transactional-email providers. Access is limited to authenticated account
owners and authorized service operators.

Users can delete their account and associated exams, scans, and results from
the Profile screen. For access, correction, deletion, or privacy questions,
contact the support address published on the Assessly store listing.

This document must be reviewed by the publisher, updated with the legal entity,
support email, hosting regions, retention periods, and applicable jurisdiction,
then hosted at a stable public HTTPS URL before store submission.
