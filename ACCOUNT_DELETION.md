# Delete an Assessly account

Users can delete an account directly in the Assessly app:

1. Sign in.
2. Open **Profile**.
3. Select **Delete account**.
4. Read the warning and select **Delete permanently**.

Deletion removes the account and its exams, answer keys, scans, detected
answers, scoring rules, and results. The operation cannot be undone. Uploaded
images are not retained after processing.

The publisher must host this page at a stable public HTTPS URL and enter that
URL in Google Play Console. Add a verified support contact and an assisted
deletion process here before publication for users who cannot access the app.
